Chocolate Cake Recipe

Learn how to make a delicious,soft and moist chocolate cake at home with these simple ingredients and easy steps

Ingredients

  • 2-cups all purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180 degree celcius
  2. Mix flour,sugar,cocoa powder,and baking powder in a bowl
  3. Add eggs,milk and melted butter
  4. Mix all the ingredients until the batter is smooth.
  5. Pour the batter into a greased cake pan.
  6. Bake for 30-35 minutes.
  7. Allow the cake to cool before serving
